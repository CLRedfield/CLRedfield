## 关于我 / About Me

我喜欢探索有趣的游戏规则，尤其是策略、卡牌、棋盘与肉鸽之间的组合。从一个小点子开始，逐步完成规则、交互与可玩的版本，是我很享受的过程。  
I enjoy exploring game mechanics, especially combinations of strategy, cards, board games, and roguelike progression. I like starting with a small idea and developing its rules, interactions, and playable build.

目前正在探索独立游戏开发与游戏创业，也在学习如何与同学协作，把作品打磨得更完整。希望做出有辨识度、值得反复游玩的游戏。  
I'm exploring indie game development and entrepreneurship, while learning to collaborate with fellow students and polish projects. I hope to make distinctive games that people want to return to.

## 我关注的方向 / What I Explore

- **游戏设计 / Game Design** — 规则组合、策略选择、卡组构筑与成长系统。 / Mechanics, strategic choices, deckbuilding, and progression.
- **玩法实现 / Playable Experiences** — 浏览器游戏、交互界面、本地 AI 与好友联机。 / Browser games, interactive interfaces, local AI, and friend multiplayer.
- **持续打磨 / Iteration** — 从原型到可玩版本，完善教学、反馈与整体体验。 / Developing prototypes into playable builds with clearer tutorials, feedback, and a more cohesive experience.
